Pave it · Repair it · Seal it · Resurface it · Restore it

Tar and Chip

Our most popular residential surface. Hot tar goes down and gets covered in stone, giving you a durable driveway with a natural look for less than full asphalt. Holds up well on long lanes and country roads, and we can run it in weather that shuts down most asphalt work.

Driveway Stone

Fresh stone, regrading, filling ruts and potholes. Good work all year round, and the right call when you want the driveway solid and smooth without the cost of paving. Winter is a fine time for it.

Asphalt and Blacktop

New driveways, replacements, and widening. Laid and compacted properly so it doesn't go to pieces in a few seasons.

Sealcoating

Seals the surface against water, salt, and sun so you get years more out of a driveway you already paid for.

Crack Repair

Hot-applied crack filling that keeps water out of the base — which is what actually destroys a driveway. We can do this year round as long as it's dry.

Resurfacing & Restoration

If the base is still sound, we can put a new surface over the top instead of tearing it out. Usually a good deal less money than starting over.

We buy local

In order to help keep our local economy thriving, around 90% of all our products and materials are purchased either directly or through distributors right here in the Shenandoah Valley. It means faster turnaround on your job, and it means the money stays in the Valley instead of leaving it.

How long does a new driveway take?

Most residential driveways are a one or two day job. We'll give you a target date when we write the estimate, and we'll call you if weather moves it. We'd rather tell you straight than give you a date we can't hold.

When can I drive on it?

Stay off it for 24 hours before any vehicle. Keep heavy traffic or anything doing sharp turns off for the first 30 days while it cures. We'll leave you a card with the specifics for your job.

Can you work in cold weather?

Most of what you read online says it has to be 50 degrees or warmer. That's true for the patch material you buy in a bucket at the store. Our equipment lets us control the temperature and make up for the cold, so we can do crack repair year round as long as it's dry, and we run stone and tar and chip work later into the season than most. It comes down to the temperature we can hold, not the month on the calendar.

Will you come out for a small job?

Yes. Short driveways, single patches, one load of stone — we do that work all the time and we're glad to. Call and ask.

What's the difference between tar and chip and asphalt?

Asphalt is a smooth, solid surface laid hot and rolled. Tar and chip is hot tar covered in stone, so you get a textured finish that looks more natural and costs less. Tar and chip also holds up well on long lanes and grades. We'll tell you honestly which one suits your driveway.

How does your pricing compare to other contractors?

Very competitive. We're not trying to be the cheapest outfit in the Valley — we're priced fairly, we tell you the number up front, and we show up when we said we would.

Do you do commercial work?

We specialize in residential driveways. That's where we do our best work and it's what we want to be known for.

How far do you travel?

The Shenandoah Valley and the counties around it. If you're wondering whether we come your way, call and ask — we've driven farther than people expect for the right job.

Do I need to be home during the job?

Not necessarily, but we do a walk-through with you when the job is finished before payment is due.

How quickly do you respond to inquiries?

We aim to respond within 24 hours. Most estimates are written on-site while we're there, or within 24 hours.
